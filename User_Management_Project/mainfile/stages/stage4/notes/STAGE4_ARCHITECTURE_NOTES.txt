--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Range Mapping Formula:
  Higher CIBIL score (closer to 900) yields favorable (lower) interest rate.
  Lower CIBIL score (closer to 600) yields higher interest rate.
    normalized = (900.0 - cibilScore) / 300.0; // 0.0 at 900, 1.0 at 600
    calculatedRate = minRate + normalized * (maxRate - minRate);
